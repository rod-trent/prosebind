# One

The quarry had been dry since the spring. Elena walked the rim of it, counting the
places where the rock had given way. Her grey eyes followed the line of the water
mark, and she thought about how long it had taken to get here.

Marcus said nothing for a long while. He had driven up from the coast that morning
and had not yet taken off his coat.

"You knew," he said at last. "About the letter."

She did not answer him. Aunt Ruth had warned her about this, years ago, in a
kitchen that smelled of scorched milk.
