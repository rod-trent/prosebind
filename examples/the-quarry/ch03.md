# Three

Elena drives back to the quarry alone. She parks at the gate and walks the last
stretch, and the wind comes hard off the water, and the gulls turn above her in
the grey light of the afternoon that is going.

Elena was 38 years old and had never once been afraid of this place.

"You always did come back here," Marcus said, falling into step beside her.

Elena looked at him. Her green eyes were wet, though the wind was reason
enough for that.

I never understood why she went back. The rest of us had the sense to stay away
from the water after what happened, but she never did learn that particular
lesson, and there was nothing any of us could say to her about it.
